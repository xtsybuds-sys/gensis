Genesis

This is the protocell baseline: a live, well-mixed chemistry that started from monomers and energy, with no cells placed. Every vesicle is a real protocell from the reactor; its size, lineage and catalyst count come from the simulation.

The shoreline, hills and vents are an illustrative setting for the live chemistry, not simulated terrain. Vesicle positions are presentation-only because the reactor is well-mixed; they are spread for visibility, not to claim a spatial model. The sun is real geometry.
